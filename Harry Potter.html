What is it

Harry Potter is a fantasy book series written by J.K. Rowling that follows the life of a young wizard, Harry Potter, and his adventures at the Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. The story centers around Harry’s battle against the dark wizard Lord Voldemort, who seeks to take over the magical world. Along with his close friends, Hermione Granger and Ron Weasley, Harry uncovers secrets about his past, learns powerful magic, and faces challenges that test his courage and loyalty. The series blends themes of friendship, bravery, and the fight between good and evil.

Harry Potter

Harry Potter is the main character in the Harry Potter series by J.K. Rowling. He is a young wizard who discovers on his eleventh birthday that he has magical powers and is invited to attend Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. Orphaned as a baby when the dark wizard Lord Voldemort killed his parents, Harry learns that he is famous in the wizarding world for surviving Voldemort’s attack. Brave, loyal, and determined, Harry forms strong friendships and faces many dangers as he grows up, ultimately leading the fight to defeat Voldemort and protect the magical world.

Ron Weasely

Ron Weasley is one of Harry Potter’s best friends and a key character in the Harry Potter series. He comes from a large, loving wizarding family and is known for his loyalty, bravery, and sense of humor. Although he sometimes struggles with self-confidence, Ron proves himself time and again as a true friend and a courageous ally in the fight against dark magic. He supports Harry and Hermione through countless challenges at Hogwarts and beyond, and his strong heart and deep loyalty make him an essential part of their journey.

Hermione Granger

Hermione Granger is one of the main characters in the Harry Potter series, known for her intelligence, determination, and loyalty. She is a Muggle-born witch, meaning she was born to non-magical parents, but quickly proves herself as one of the brightest students at Hogwarts. Hermione is a close friend of Harry Potter and Ron Weasley, often using her quick thinking and deep knowledge of spells to help them out of difficult situations. Brave and principled, she stands up for what is right, even when it’s unpopular, and plays a key role in the fight against Lord Voldemort.

FUN FACTS

Facts from the Wizarding World and Behind the scenes

🧙‍♂️ Wizarding World Fun Facts

The Hogwarts motto is in Latin. “Draco dormiens nunquam titillandus” means “Never tickle a sleeping dragon.”

J.K. Rowling and Harry share a birthday. Both were born on July 31st!

Dumbledore is an old English word for "bumblebee." Rowling imagined him humming to himself a lot.

Ron almost died. J.K. Rowling considered killing Ron in the middle of the series during a dark time in her life.

Voldemort's name is French. “Vol de mort” roughly translates to “flight of death” or “theft of death.”

🧹 Behind-the-Scenes Movie Magic

Daniel Radcliffe went through 160 pairs of glasses. And about 80 wands during the filming!

The cast had homework. Alfonso Cuarón (director of Prisoner of Azkaban) made the trio write essays in character. Emma Watson wrote a 16-page essay. Rupert Grint didn’t turn his in—just like Ron !

The food in the Great Hall scenes was real. But it started rotting under the lights, so later films used resin models.

Tom Felton (Draco) had to get his pockets sewn shut. Because he kept sneaking food from set!.

Hedwig wasn’t just one owl. Over seven owls played Hedwig throughout the films.
